      <h2>Spintronics & spin-orbit phenomena</h2>
      <p>I investigate current-induced spin responses, transport properties, and spin-orbit torques in realistic materials. The goal is to understand how crystal symmetry, electronic structure, and spin-orbit coupling shape experimentally measurable response tensors.</p>
      <p>Typical tools include density-functional theory, Wannier tight-binding models, Kubo response theory, and semiclassical transport calculations.</p>

      <p class="eyebrow">02</p>
      <h2>Noncollinear & topological spin textures</h2>
      <p>A major theme of my research is the microscopic origin of noncollinear magnetic states in two-dimensional magnets, including skyrmions, spin spirals, and frustration-driven spin textures. I combine realistic magnetic parameters with atomistic spin simulations to connect electronic structure to emergent magnetic phases.</p>

      <p class="eyebrow">03</p>
      <h2>Multiferroics & magnetoelectricity</h2>
      <p>I study spin-induced ferroelectricity and magnetoelectric coupling in low-dimensional magnetic materials. A central question is how magnetic order breaks spatial symmetries and produces electric polarization, and how this coupling can be detected or controlled through electronic and optical responses.</p>
