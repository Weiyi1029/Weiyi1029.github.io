      <h1>Dr. Weiyi Pan (潘伟屹)</h1>

      <div class="bio-copy">
        <p>My name is Weiyi Pan, and I am a scientist in theoretical condensed matter physics and computational materials science. I am currently a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Physics at the <a href="https://www.uni-regensburg.de/" target="_blank" rel="noopener" style="text-decoration: underline;">University of Regensburg</a>, Germany. Before joining Regensburg, I received my Ph.D. in Physics from <a href="https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/" target="_blank" rel="noopener" style="text-decoration: underline;">Tsinghua University</a> in 2025, after earning my B.Sc. in Physics from <a href="https://english.hust.edu.cn/" target="_blank" rel="noopener" style="text-decoration: underline;">Huazhong University of Science and Technology</a> in 2020.</p>

        <p>My research focuses on spintronic phenomena in solids, with the aid of first-principles calculations and theoretical modeling. I am particularly interested in emergent magnetic phenomena in low-dimensional materials, such as magnetic skyrmions and magnetically induced ferroelectricity, as well as spin transport and dynamics, including charge-to-spin conversion and spin-orbit torques. I also work closely with experimental collaborators to understand and explore novel spin-related phenomena in real materials.</p>

        <p>Beyond physics, I am a history enthusiast and enjoy discovering different landscapes, cultures, and places through travel. So far, I have visited more than ten countries.</p>
